For parents & teachers

A simple guide to supporting learning at home and in school.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent guide

  • Help children follow a calm, regular study routine.
  • Encourage questions and curiosity rather than memorising only.
  • Review completed worksheets together.
  • Celebrate effort, kindness and improvement.
  • Keep screen use balanced with rest and offline play.

🧑‍🏫 Teacher guide

  • Use chapter notes as a starting point, not a replacement for prescribed textbooks.
  • Adapt examples to students' level and local context.
  • Use quizzes as low-stakes checks for understanding.
  • Review written work and provide constructive feedback.
  • Check the latest CBSE/NCERT syllabus before assigning content.

About EduNova

EduNova is an independent learning resource designed to support students in Classes 1–10. It is not an official CBSE or NCERT website. Content should be checked against the school's prescribed books and current curriculum.

Safety note: This demo does not collect student names, contact details or account information. Quiz scores remain in the local browser.
